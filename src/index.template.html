AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL PROBABLY ONLY

Start with Full Manikin Rounds on Curriculum review. Standard cases contain eight distinct learning targets. Five-question and ten-question settings are also available. The focused modules use the same content standards within a narrower topic.

Read the question before selecting. You may click a structure, identify a highlighted structure, type an anatomical term or number, interpret a report, or classify a specimen and justify the category. Classification plus its reason is one question.

Clear diagrams are not answer clues. The same full regional view stays in place. Enlarge opens that entire view; only you choose whether to zoom further. Brackets in the long-bone drawing indicate gross regions, not separate tissues.

Practice: retries and explanations are unlimited. A hint or revealed answer marks the item as supported rather than independent first-try recall. Game: first-try completion earns 100 points; after one error, 60; after two or more, 30. Errors deduct 20, with score floored at zero. Reviewing an answer earns no points. Both parts of a classification question must be correct.

Coverage: the planner avoids duplicated learning targets and related concept groups within a case. It prioritizes unused eligible targets across cases. When a balanced case needs a previously covered target, the item is explicitly marked REVIEW. New wording alone never counts as a new target.

Main Menu pauses rather than erases a case. Use Resume to continue, even after reopening the same file in the same browser when local storage is available. Practice is open-ended. A scored shift contains four manikin cases. Sound and the optional timer default to off.

Deliberate exclusions and qualifications

Cell-level anatomy and bone repair/remodeling are outside this edition: there are no questions or atlas fields on those topics. Also excluded: unavailable video diagnoses, invented interactive-model conclusions, a universal femur-strength value and an exact newborn count. Growth-plate timing is not assessed; the epiphyseal plate remains a location-only model label. Body-region and bone-count conventions retained from v3 are qualified where needed.

Rib terminology: questions distinguish true pairs 1-7, indirectly attached non-floating pairs 8-10, and floating pairs 11-12. Where FALSE is used inclusively it means pairs 8-12, with floating ribs as its subset. That answer-key convention is explicit because the worksheet itself leaves the definitions blank.

Art and scale: The v3 complete long-bone model and the remaining regional artwork are preserved. Periosteum follows the non-articular exterior, not the artificial cut surface. Marrow is partly removed to show the cavity. No target-specific automatic crop or extra small-scale atlas field is used.
